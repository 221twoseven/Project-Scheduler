## Slide 1

**Title:** Two Seven: Shop Timeline

The Shop Timeline is a Gantt planning app, project calendar, and forecasting tool.
You can create and manage project schedules, teams and departments with it.

The Shop Timeline is a company-wide system. It is a **shared source of truth**
for our project, client, personnel, availability and scheduling data previously
spread across several documents.

---

## Slide 2

**Title:** Enter it once

The Shop Timeline is intended to put project tracking information in one place that's 
visible to project contributors.

This app is connected to our company data in Microsoft 365.
**Enter information once, maintain it in one place, and it becomes useful everywhere.**

When a project date changes, it changes on every schedule, calendar and dashboard.

---

## Slide 3

**Title:** Who it's for

**Management** — the company-wide view: what's happening, what's coming, where
resources are committed, where we may have capacity or staffing issues.

**Project Managers** — where a project enters the schedule: the timeline,
milestones and dates other departments plan around.

**Design** — a planning and working tool: what we're responsible for,
when, and how it's distributed.

**Shop & production** — visibility: what's coming, when it moves through the shop,
who's involved.

These aren't four separate schedules. Everyone sees **different views of the same
information**.

---

## Slide 4

**Title:** What it is/isn't

This is not an ERP (Enterprise Resource Planner).
It does not replace estimating, accounting, purchasing or HR systems. 

It exists to answer four questions: 
**What are we working on? 
When is it happening?
Who is working on it? 
What does everyone need to know to plan around it?**

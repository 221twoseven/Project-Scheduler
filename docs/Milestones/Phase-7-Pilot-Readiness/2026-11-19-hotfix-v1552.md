# v1.55.2 — a hotfix from `main`: Pin checkbox, milestone hover, milestones on subtasks

**Date:** 2026-11-19 · **Items:** 96, 97 · **App:** v1.55.2 (production)

## What changed
- **Pin checkbox** (phase details): sits beside its words. A shared rule stretched every
  input in that panel to full width, so the box floated alone under the End date.
- **Milestone hover** (timeline): hovering a diamond shows that milestone alone — name,
  date, project, note. It used to show the whole phase's tooltip. The "?" help cursor is gone.
- **Milestones on subtasks** (project page): a milestone's phase list also names the bars
  of any department with more than one. Picking a bar moves the milestone onto that bar's
  row (`ticketNodes` in `ShopTimeline_Tasks`); picking the department moves it back to
  `ShopTimeline_Events`. No new SharePoint column. My Dashboard lists both kinds.

## Why it shipped from `main`
`development` holds the Office work and isn't ready to promote (owner, 2026-11-19). The
fix was built on `main` (v1.55.2) and carried into `development` by a second PR, without a
version of its own there; its CHANGELOG entry sits between v1.55.1 and v1.56.0.

## Ceilings
- A new-project draft still picks a department only; subtasks are offered once saved.
- Dragging a subtask's milestone onto another department's row makes it a department
  milestone there (same as picking that department).

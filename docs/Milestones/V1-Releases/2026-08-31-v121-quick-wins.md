# v1.2.1 — 08-31 quick wins (first batch of the second owner brief)

**Date:** 2026-08-31 · **Version:** v1.2.1 (patch) · **Branch:** `development` → `/preview/`
**Objectives:** 08-31 brief objs 12, 2, 10, 11, 1 (TODO §3 items 14–18)

## What changed

1. **"Add a phase" left the right-click menu** (obj 12). Right-clicking blank space
   on a project page now offers New checkpoint and New task only. Adding a phase
   still has two doors: double-click blank calendar space (the v1.1.0 picker), or
   tick the department in the editor's Departments list.
2. **Past projects lost their LATE pill** (obj 2). The red "LATE 42d" chip in the
   sidebar now shows only while a job is still current — some bar ends today or
   later. A project whose work wrapped and whose install date is behind us stays
   quiet; it wasn't news anymore.
3. **The overlap warning counts jobs** (obj 10). The bar tooltip's
   "⚠ Nick is on another job during this window" now says "…is on **2 other
   jobs**…" — the real count of other projects that person is booked on during
   that window, singular/plural handled.
4. **Forecast projects are uncolored** (obj 11, owner global rule). A penciled-in
   job draws grey (`#6B7484`) everywhere — bars in both color modes (install bars
   included), sidebar dot, dashed outline — on top of its existing faded/dashed
   treatment. Its palette slot stays reserved, so when the status firms up the hue
   comes back without shuffling anyone else's color. Legend and Design-Language
   §2.1 updated: this is the one exception to "hue is identity at every status."
5. **The tour teaches the date-bar drag** (obj 1). New "Slide through time" step
   on the main tour, right after the timeline step — click-drag the numbered date
   bar to pan, T/Today to come back, G to jump. (New steps aren't covered by the
   coach-copy hold, which the owner tabled indefinitely the same day.)

## Why it mattered

All five were owner asks from the 2026-08-31 brief — noise reduction (2, 11, 12),
truth in warnings (10), and discoverability for the least-findable navigation move
in the app (1).

## v1.2.2 — dashboard presentation (same day, owner brief)

My Dashboard stops presenting as a filtered view. The person that mechanically
powers it (Departments lens + person filter) no longer counts as a filter while the
dashboard is on: no "Person: name" chip (the trail bar already says who), no Filters
badge for it, the Person section hidden from the Filters menu (status + client
only), Clear filters hidden until a real status/client filter is set — and clearing
never exits the dashboard. The empty-state card there stops explaining a "person
filter" and points at the × instead. Off the dashboard, the person filter is
unchanged.

## Correction — v1.2.2 (same day)

Obj 2's "date pill" turned out to be the **B1 off-screen edge chip** on the Gantt
canvas (owner screenshot), not the sidebar LATE chip. v1.2.2 reverted the LATE-chip
gating — it renders again exactly as before v1.2.1 — and instead stopped issuing the
**left** edge chip for rows whose every bar ended before today. A current project
scrolled out of view still gets its chip; right-side chips always point at upcoming
bars and were never touched.
